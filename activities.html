ADM: Art and Design Magazine

Founded in 2025 as the Visual Academy’s own art and design magazine. A team of 15 to 20 students writes, photographs, and designs each issue; Cara is Editor-in-Chief and art directs the whole thing, from the cover to the type. Read the first issue (32 pages, PDF) ↗

Krafty Kids Workshop, Palisades Park Library

Since 2023 Cara has taught a free biweekly art and craft class for children at the public library, 110 volunteer hours so far. The children’s pieces become murals on the library walls.
